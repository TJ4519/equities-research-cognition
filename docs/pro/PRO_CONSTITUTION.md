# PRO Constitution

Status: governing product intent and authority boundary. This document does not claim that the present repository implements the mature product.

Adopted by the repository-grounded PRO owner on 17 August 2026 from the verified handoff at commit `c1fc568424facbb5bb8e1b6369d30a1f380ae308`.

## Product centre

The product is an ongoing analyst modelling-and-research environment in which agents create useful work while the exact evidence, calculations, assumptions, transformations, dependencies, corrections, and permission to rely on each resulting artifact remain reconstructable.

Its economic purpose is to preserve the leverage of agent-assisted research as use expands across more companies, episodes, and people without forcing analysts to rebuild every output by hand. The product should make consequential errors containable and professional judgment attributable while leaving the analyst in control of contested meaning and use.

The mature product is not defined by the current Django pages, campaign vocabulary, agent roster, workbenches, NTM panes, Langfuse traces, or spreadsheet fixture. Those are candidate means and implementation evidence. Product form remains open among an integrated modelling-and-research workspace, an artifact-native companion to existing professional tools, and a durable research-job environment with specialised workspaces.

Excel is an economically important native artifact. It is not the universal ontology of the product.

## User and practitioner evidence

The immediate user of this repository is a technical operator and applied researcher working outside their own sell-side expertise. The product must therefore preserve where claims came from and must not convert the operator's synthesis into practitioner validation.

The practitioner signal came from one sophisticated analyst through the user's mentor. The analyst reported useful frontier-model work across model building and updating, earnings work, statistical analysis, visualisation, thesis consistency, management-narrative comparison, and institutional memory. This supports the proposition that serious agent-assisted research practice is emerging. It does not establish a universal task taxonomy, a product roadmap, the superiority of the observed methods, or analyst approval of this product.

No public artifact may expose excluded private identities, communications, research, credentials, traces, service identifiers, or protected cases.

## Causal business logic

1. Expert analysts are already inventing agent-assisted methods that save time or improve reach.
2. When a method becomes useful, analysts run it more often, over more companies, and eventually across teams.
3. Greater volume increases retrievals, transformations, calculations, handoffs, and downstream consumers.
4. A plausible error can then propagate farther before discovery, while exhaustive human reconstruction can erase the leverage that justified the agent.
5. A valuable product must therefore preserve useful autonomy while making consequential support, change, uncertainty, dependency, correction, and artifact use reconstructable.
6. Repeated expert corrections may become cumulative organisational improvement only when they are anchored to exact episodes, converted into protected evaluations, and promoted through versioned, reversible workflow changes.

This is the governing commercial thesis. It remains a commercial bet until a serious analyst uses a real product path and the resulting evidence shows that the product saves net effort, contains consequential mistakes, and supports a repeatable adoption path.

## First counterexample

The first proof centres on model population because a value can be numerically plausible and still be inadmissible for a particular target.

An agent may retrieve the right-looking number from the wrong document class, entity, reporting period, units, accounting basis, or economic scope. If that value enters a workbook, formulas and valuation outputs may propagate it. A polished answer, correct arithmetic, a valid schema, or an agent's confidence does not repair the source-to-target mismatch.

Admissibility is target-specific. The product must not encode the slogan that GAAP always wins. Legitimate targets may require non-GAAP measures, derived calculations, estimates, or analyst assumptions. Machine-owned records must bind exact source bytes and declared source attributes to an explicit target contract. Known invalid states must fail closed. Missing or contested meaning must remain provisional for analyst judgment.

## Authority split

### Agents

Agents may interpret an objective, search, compare, calculate, challenge, synthesise, and propose candidate artifacts or bounded refusals. Their outputs are proposals. Agent completion cannot grant research authority.

### Schemas, prompts, protocols, and skills

Schemas make handoffs inspectable. Prompts and protocols shape model behaviour. Executable skills can make operations repeatable. None of these establishes source identity, semantic truth, professional materiality, or permission to rely on a result.

### Host software

Deterministic host software owns identity, exact bytes, versions, timestamps, evidence cutoffs, permissions, runtime entitlements, known admissibility predicates, dependency relations, legal state transitions, immutable chronology, and fail-closed enforcement. Host checks may establish that a declared condition holds; they may not manufacture contested professional meaning.

### Analysts

Analysts own professional ambiguity, assumptions, materiality, corrections, and permission to rely on one exact artifact for one named use. Authority must be scoped to the artifact, use, commission, scenario, and release state rather than represented by a generic acceptance flag.

### Observability

Langfuse and other telemetry may explain execution and correlate traces with canonical state. Observability is diagnostic evidence, not research evidence and not an authority mechanism.

## Default state and promotion rule

Every agent-produced result begins as candidate state. If required source attributes, target contracts, entitlement facts, dependencies, or exact inputs are absent or ambiguous, the result remains quarantined or the operation refuses.

No prompt instruction, model output, trace, score, schema validation, test result, or generic approval may silently promote a candidate into institutional truth. Promotion requires the deterministic conditions declared for that object and the required scoped human act.

A configured policy may change only through a versioned, attributable, reviewable update with a defined scope and rollback condition. Model output cannot modify the live policy that judges it.

## Evidence discipline

Every durable claim must carry one of these statuses:

- **Observed practitioner signal:** reported practice from the sophisticated analyst; useful as discovery evidence, not validation of this product.
- **Current software fact:** directly established by inspected code, migrations, tests, or a preserved live receipt at a named commit.
- **Inference:** a conclusion supported by observed evidence or software facts but not directly demonstrated.
- **Extension:** a proposed mechanism or product behaviour derived from the thesis and counterexamples.
- **Commercial bet:** a proposition whose truth depends on analyst usefulness, adoption, economics, or transfer across teams.
- **Unresolved:** a question for which present evidence is insufficient.

Mechanical tests may prove parsing, arithmetic, digests, permissions, transitions, transport, rendering, and hostile-case containment. They cannot prove research quality, cognition, topology quality, analyst usefulness, product fit, deployment readiness, or institutional improvement.

A manual research report created outside the product is a quality reference only. It cannot be attached after the fact and counted as a product-generated episode.

## Analyst-facing product rules

The analyst should encounter recognisable work: the company and question, current model or research artifact, evidence beside the value or claim it informs, material assumptions and methods, proposed changes, downstream consequences, unresolved judgment, resulting artifacts, collaboration, and resumption.

Backend roles, stages, state-machine terms, and ontology must not become the analyst's vocabulary by default. Richness must come from useful work and native artifacts, not ornamental panels. Analyst-facing designs must not use eyebrows, overlines, kickers, supratitles, decorative categorical labels, or controls without a source of truth and consequence.

Every visible fact and action must map to canonical backend state, a legal transition, a validation path, and an honest support status.

## Anti-goals

The project must not become:

- a chat wrapper presented as a research environment;
- a provenance dashboard detached from useful analyst work;
- a static Excel review page that does not open, recalculate, or change a workbook;
- a visible multi-agent graph or pane roster mistaken for the analyst workflow;
- a dossier or casebook that exposes invented backend terminology;
- a fixture-driven demo or imported manual report presented as live success;
- a prompt-only attempt to enforce source identity or target admissibility;
- a system that treats every correction as a reusable lesson;
- a live self-modifying workflow without protected comparison and rollback; or
- an Agentic SDLC transplant that allows a novel framework to select the product architecture.

## Demo release threshold

A result may be called a product demo only when one ordinary authenticated path establishes all of the following:

1. tenant-correct persistence;
2. an exact analyst objective, starting artifact, evidence cutoff, permitted sources, material assumptions, and named decision use;
3. real NTM/Codex work initiated from canonical product state;
4. useful agent-produced research or modelling work rather than imported fixture content;
5. machine-owned evidence identity and target-specific checks;
6. an intentional correct-looking but inadmissible input contained before it acquires downstream authority;
7. inspectable support and consequences in ordinary analyst language;
8. an exact analyst correction or decision followed by correct recomputation or bounded refusal;
9. one exact resulting artifact with lineage and restart retrieval;
10. diagnostic traces that agree with canonical state but cannot grant authority; and
11. explicit nonclaims and analyst questions capable of producing go, revise, or kill.

The hypothesis must be killed or redesigned when analysts must reconstruct most of the work, review consumes the saved time, source rules overblock legitimate work, workbook support remains bespoke to one episode, or the system cannot safely separate web identity, runtime entitlement, and research authority.

## Governed learning

An expert correction must remain bound to the exact input, proposal, policy version, artifact version, and human action that caused it. The correction may seed an evaluation candidate and then a candidate prompt, skill, tool, or deterministic gate change.

Promotion requires comparison against the triggering case and protected different cases, with false admission, false blocking, analyst burden, cost, and latency measured. A promoted change must have a name, scope, approver, and rollback condition. Until later comparison, the strongest permitted claim is that the product captured and applied an authorised experiment, not that the system learned or improved.

## Operating consequence

Work proceeds in causal order: preserve this constitution; establish exact repository and runtime truth; audit active cognition and authority paths; choose one falsifiable product hypothesis; write an active build contract; issue bounded implementation packets; inspect diffs and execute the live path; then evaluate governed learning. Product form, prompt rewrites, additional agents, and donor frameworks remain downstream of that ground.
