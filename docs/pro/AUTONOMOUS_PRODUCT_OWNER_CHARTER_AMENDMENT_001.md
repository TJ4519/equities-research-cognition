# Autonomous Product Owner Charter Amendment 001

Status: governing amendment activated by the user's end-to-end delivery commission on 17 August 2026.

## Controlling change

The previous charter correctly withheld implementation while the joined product system had not survived hostile review. The user has now granted standing authority to move beyond passive Level 1 after that review and to continue through bounded implementation, verification and internally scoped experimental promotion without routine human approval.

The freeze is therefore replaced by a gated continuation rule:

```text
Milestone 0 authority reconciliation
-> Milestone 1 independent hostile vertical adjudication
-> accepted or repaired outcome-complete vertical
-> Level 2 bounded vertical orchestration
-> implementation, hostile verification and workflow testing
-> Level 3 autonomous repository delivery when its evidence is satisfied
```

No milestone may be skipped by confidence, documentation or implementation momentum.

## Autonomy level

Current status after this amendment is adopted:

- authority grant: activated;
- present programme state: `CONTINUING`;
- active milestone: Milestone 1 hostile vertical adjudication;
- current demonstrated autonomy: Level 1 architecture ownership plus authority to progress automatically after the hostile-review gate;
- Level 2 becomes active only when an independent review clears or repairs one outcome-complete vertical and the decision ledger records the accepted contract;
- Level 3 remains evidence-gated by one working, independently verified repository vertical with restart, rollback and truthful product behaviour.

The owner must not describe the authority grant itself as evidence that Level 2 or Level 3 has been earned.

## Product-owner duty to continue

After a milestone closes, the owner must either:

- activate the next authorised milestone;
- enter `REPAIRING` with an owned diagnosis, repair, rerun and re-review path;
- enter `BLOCKED_ON_HUMAN_AUTHORITY` after all safe work is complete and one exact decision is identified;
- reach `VERIFIED_V0_READY_FOR_COLD_TEST`; or
- reach `CLIENT_READY` after the separate professional and production gates.

`Documents complete`, `review complete`, `worker finished`, `PR open`, `tests green`, `demo rendered`, `architecture coherent` and `nothing else in the packet` remain invalid terminal states.

## Human-only authority retained

The activated continuation grant does not delegate:

- protected or client data access;
- permanent credentials or account administration;
- API consent or external sharing;
- material expenditure or contract commitment;
- analyst or client contact;
- external production release;
- legal, regulatory, publication or investment judgment;
- professional permission to rely on an artifact; or
- production-tenant harness promotion.

The owner may prepare and verify public or synthetic work up to the corresponding gate.

## Full-handoff readiness update

The governing surfaces now support an autonomous delivery programme, but the product-delivery evidence is still incomplete. Full delivery autonomy requires:

- accepted hostile review of one outcome-complete vertical;
- fresh-context reconstitution;
- bounded implementation packets with stable interfaces;
- one joined login-to-candidate-or-refusal product path;
- deterministic containment of a plausible invalid proposal;
- exact correction, candidate artifact, recalculation and scoped use mechanics;
- independent hostile verification;
- truthful cold product evidence;
- restart and stale-context recovery;
- executable rollback; and
- a release manifest reconciling code, migrations, tests, docs, ledger and checkpoint.

## Supersession

This amendment supersedes only the charter statements that current authority ends at passive Level 1 and that no implementation may proceed after a hostile review without a fresh routine permission request. It preserves every evidence, delegation, product-design, AI-engineering, claim, escalation and human-authority constraint not explicitly changed here.
