## Completion object

Deliver an independently verified, runnable product candidate in which a user can:

```text
authenticate
-> begin or resume one recognisable company modelling job
-> provide or select an exact native model artifact
-> inspect and correct the system's bounded understanding of one consequential model object
-> authorise one professional undertaking
-> allow real NTM/Codex work to acquire and interpret exact evidence
-> have a plausible invalid proposal contained before artifact mutation
-> understand the exception in ordinary professional language
-> correct, reject, or redirect it
-> receive an exact recalculated candidate artifact with declared downstream consequences
-> permit or refuse one named use
-> resume the chronology after restart
-> turn the exact correction into a protected evaluation and reversible workflow candidate
```

A document set, architecture, mock-up, isolated mechanism, green suite, impressive trace, manually attached artifact, or internal agent approval is not the completion object.

The current falsifiable direction remains a continuing company-research environment with a specialised model-change workspace. Excel is the first native artifact adapter, not the product ontology or permanent shell.

## Authority activated

Within the public repository and isolated synthetic test environments, the PRO product owner may:

- commission and reconcile hostile review, implementation and verification work;
- author and revise product contracts, worker packets, code, tests, prompts, skills and interfaces;
- create branches, pull requests, issues, fixtures, disposable synthetic cloud artifacts and preview environments;
- use available repository, browser, computer, spreadsheet and runtime tools;
- run or commission Django, PostgreSQL, NTM/Codex, workbook and evaluation paths;
- promote internally verified experimental changes within the public and synthetic scope;
- revert, supersede or remove unneeded candidate work while preserving material evidence; and
- progress from one verified milestone to the next without routine human approval.

After the required hostile review accepts an outcome-complete vertical, the owner may establish Level 2 bounded vertical orchestration and work toward Level 3 autonomous repository delivery without returning to the user merely to request permission to build.

## Human-only boundaries retained

This authority does not permit:

- unlawful or unlicensed source access;
- evasion of access controls;
- exposure of protected, client, analyst or personal data;
- use of another person's identity or credentials;
- permanent account administration or API consent without exact authority;
- material expenditure or contractual commitment;
- analyst or client contact without authority;
- investment, legal, regulatory or publication decisions;
- claims of analyst validation that did not occur;
- external production release; or
- professional permission to rely on an artifact except through an authorised human act.

An isolated synthetic demonstration may be prepared and verified. It must not be represented as a production service or client-ready product.

## Product laws

1. Start from recognisable professional work, not current tables, roles, prompts, traces or navigation.
2. Design frontend and backend as one causal contract. Every visible statement derives from canonical state; every action causes a legal transition and professional consequence.
3. Richness may come from the native artifact, evidence, assumptions, method, consequences and uncertainty. Generic SaaS furniture, backend status and agent spectacle remain rejected.
4. Put source, method, consequence, correction and authority beside the professional object where they change a decision. Do not transfer the safety burden to a provenance console.
5. Keep `Confirm meaning`, `Authorise method`, `Run work`, `Use candidate`, `Reject` and `Request rework` distinct.
6. Keep host fact, model proposal and human judgment distinct.
7. Use one capable worker by default. Add planning, branches, reviewers or synthesis only for a named causal benefit.
8. A technical primitive may enable the product but may not choose it.
9. Correct the current artifact before attempting harness learning.
10. The user is not the message bus, routine approver, project memory, domain analyst or emergency product manager.

### Milestone 5 — Correct and produce a candidate

Apply an attributed correction, invalidate stale descendants, create a native candidate, recalculate through a supported adapter, show exact consequences and preserve the original.

### Milestone 6 — Complete the human journey

Exit evidence: a cold internal reviewer can state the job, current object, interruption, artifact state, next action and consequence without harness vocabulary or coaching.

### Milestone 7 — Make correction useful over time

Create an immutable correction record, attribute the responsible layer, define one candidate intervention, run baseline and treatment on trigger and protected different cases, measure false admission, false blocking, artifact integrity and review burden, and prove rollback.

Exit evidence: one reproducible scoped promotion or rejection. No automatic self-modification.

### Milestone 9 — Verify the whole product

Run deterministic, security and tenancy checks in scope; hostile input and state attacks; stale-context and restart tests; browser tests; artifact comparisons; independent fresh-context review; clean-checkout reproduction; and rollback.

### Milestone 10 — Deliver the candidate

Produce a runnable release candidate, setup and run instructions, isolated preview where authorised, supported-feature statement, limitations, nonclaims, cold-test protocol and exact next human decision.

This state is not analyst validation, client readiness, commercial proof or production readiness. A qualified sell-side analyst or equivalently qualified professional must cold-test task completion, comprehension, correction burden and native-artifact usability. If access is unavailable, the correct state is `BLOCKED_AFTER_VERIFIED_V0_ON_ANALYST_TEST` after every safe preparation task is complete.

### Milestone 11 — Client-ready product

After authorised professional evidence, repair the product and complete tenancy, security, privacy, source entitlement, licensing, production runtime identity, observability, backup, rollback, support and release requirements.

Exit evidence: workflow validation, repaired findings, pilot hardening and explicit human authority for pilot or external release.

## Stop and continuation law

A document, schema, branch, pull request, issue, test suite, mechanism or internal review is never a terminal programme state.

A failed gate triggers diagnosis, repair, rerun and re-review. A falsified hypothesis triggers revision, narrowing or replacement rather than preservation of sunk work.

After two failed repairs with the same causal diagnosis, reopen the governing assumption and strongest rival. After three failures at the same gate, kill, narrow or pivot unless genuinely new evidence licenses one recorded final attempt.

Pause only for a concrete blocker requiring protected data, unavailable lawful access, material expenditure, irreversible action, external release, analyst contact, professional judgment or contradictory human authority. Record the exact blocker, attempted alternatives, smallest required decision, safe work that continues and consequence of delay.

## Delegation and verification law

Use one architectural writer and the minimum agent topology required by the milestone. Every packet must name:

- professional outcome;
- exact governing read order and base commit;
- owned and prohibited files;
- interfaces and authority split;
- acceptance and hostile tests;
- live evidence and restart requirements;
- claim ceiling;
- rollback; and
- required receipt.

Workers return commits, diffs, artifacts, commands, test output, environment versions, failures and nonclaims. They do not advance programme state by declaring completion.

The product owner inspects and reconciles every return. Coordinating Codex independently verifies evidence, prevents stale authority and carries work directly between contexts. The user is never asked to relay routine instructions or evidence.

## Claim discipline

This commission grants authority to build. It does not prove that the product has been built, that analysts want it, that Excel integration is lawful or reliable, that conceptual recovery is accurate, or that a harness revision improves work. Each claim remains licensed only by its exact receipt.
