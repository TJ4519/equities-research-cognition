## Verdict rules

### PASS

Only means that the observed authenticated browser surface reproducibly opened the bounded fixture, preserved declared semantics, accepted the target edit, recalculated the two dependents without manual entry, downloaded two independently inspected `.xlsx` copies, exposed a truthful bounded version/activity surface and was cleaned up.

### PARTIAL

One or more required direct operations worked, but repeatability, recalculation evidence, provenance visibility, export, semantic preservation, metadata safety, formula error or cleanup was incomplete.

### FAIL

The surface was available but could not safely or semantically complete the bounded profile.

### BLOCKED_BY_AUTHORITY

Execution required new consent, credentials, account administration, unrelated-file interaction, protected data or another human-only act. Record the smallest blocker and safe evidence obtained before stopping.
