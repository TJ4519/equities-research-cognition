# Product System Architecture V0

A model-aware sell-side product should be a continuing company research environment with specialised workspaces, beginning with one model-change workspace that joins the analyst's professional object, the native artifact, bounded agent work, deterministic containment, correction, recalculation, and exact permission to rely.

Status: PRO product-system adjudication. This document controls product form and architectural reasoning for the next proof. It is not an implemented design, analyst validation, or authority to begin frontend or backend construction.

## 1. Product centre

The product centre is not Excel, a web dashboard, a research report, an agent console, a provenance viewer, or the repository's campaign state. It is an ongoing body of company work in which an analyst repeatedly changes models, interpretations, assumptions, and research artifacts as new evidence arrives.

The first complete professional job is:

```text
an analyst has a current model and a consequential update to make
-> the system reconstructs the bounded model object affected
-> the analyst confirms or corrects its meaning and permitted method
-> the system captures exact eligible evidence
-> bounded agent work proposes a change or refusal
-> host controls contain known invalid states
-> the analyst sees only a consequential exception or a usable candidate
-> correction produces a new recalculated descendant
-> the analyst permits or rejects one exact artifact for one exact use
-> the job remains resumable when the next event arrives
```

The mature product should accommodate model building, existing-model updates, methodological adjustments, earnings work, ad hoc investigation, thesis maintenance, and institutional memory without pretending that they are one fixed pipeline. They share identity, evidence, conceptual objects, artifact versions, work episodes, decisions, and correction history. They do not necessarily share the same visible workspace or agent topology.

## 2. Business job and value mechanism

One sophisticated practitioner has reported useful frontier-model work across model building, updating, adjustments, earnings analysis, statistical work, visualisation, thesis consistency, management-narrative comparison, ad hoc questions, and institutional memory. This is evidence of demand from one practitioner, not a validated taxonomy or roadmap.

The common economic mechanism is scaling pressure:

```text
one useful agent-assisted method
-> more frequent use
-> more companies, periods, sources, transformations and collaborators
-> more plausible intermediate results and downstream consumers
-> greater propagation distance for a local error
-> either exhaustive reconstruction or governed exception review
```

A useful product must preserve the leverage while reducing the analyst's review surface. It succeeds when the analyst can trust the system to perform quiet, reversible work and is interrupted only for meaning, method, materiality, exception, amendment, or permission to rely.

A correct-looking result is dangerous when it is wrong for the professional object. Examples include:

- an exact value from an inadmissible document for the target;
- the right metric for the wrong entity, period, unit, basis, or scope;
- a valid formula expressing the wrong methodology;
- an estimate or analyst assumption presented as reported fact;
- a model change that preserves spreadsheet structure while misreading the business relationship;
- a completed agent run mistaken for permission to use its result; and
- a correction stored as feedback without producing a corrected artifact.

The product value is therefore not generic provenance. It is a shorter path from new evidence to a relied-upon professional artifact, with every consequential change reconstructable and every invalid state contained before propagation.

## 3. Strong product-form recommendation

### First falsification: specialised model-change workspace

The first falsification should be a model-change workspace centred on one existing native model. It should begin before cell mutation: the system proposes its understanding of one consequential model object, the analyst confirms or corrects that understanding, the system performs bounded work, and the analyst receives either a material exception or a recalculated candidate.

This is stronger than a standalone Excel companion because the conceptual object, source rules, method, agent work, correction, and use decision belong to the continuing company job rather than to a particular spreadsheet session.

It is stronger than a generic research hub because it completes one economically recognisable job and produces a changed artifact rather than another collection of notes and statuses.

It is narrower than a complete modelling platform because Excel remains the first native artifact adapter and the proof does not attempt to reproduce all spreadsheet interaction.

### Mature form: continuing company research environment with specialised workspaces

The most plausible mature form is an ongoing environment organised around a company, security, or mandate, with specialised workspaces appearing when the work demands them. A model-change workspace, earnings workspace, source comparison, assumption review, or thesis update can share canonical state without becoming permanent global navigation.

The analyst should be able to resume the company work and encounter the current professional object and next consequential action, not a dashboard of campaigns or a menu of agents.

### Artifact-native companion as an adapter strategy

Excel integration remains important. It is an adapter and interaction boundary, not the product theory. Desktop Excel, Excel for the Web, OOXML operations, and a calculation service may each support different parts of the product. The product must remain coherent if the calculation or editing adapter changes.

### Integrated modelling workspace as a later option

A fully integrated modelling environment becomes justified only when repeated evidence shows that the product must own interaction inside the model rather than coordinate changes around the native artifact. Building it first would combine spreadsheet replacement, model understanding, research quality, collaboration, and governance into one unidentifiable experiment.

## 4. Product-form comparison

| Form | What it makes easy | Principal risk | First-falsification suitability | Mature-product plausibility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Artifact-native Excel companion | Uses an existing model and familiar artifact; exposes bounded changes and recalculation | Excel convenience silently defines the product and conceptual model | High as an adapter-backed proof | Medium as a standalone product; high as one workspace or integration |
| Specialised modelling/research workspace | Joins model meaning, evidence, method, proposed change, exceptions, correction and artifact consequence | Can become a bespoke vertical or reproduce spreadsheet interaction prematurely | Highest for the first joined product proof | High as a recurring professional workspace |
| Continuing research-job environment with specialised workspaces | Preserves company history, multiple episodes, models, research, corrections and resumption | Can decay into a generic dashboard or job hub without one completed workspace | Medium initially; too broad without a concrete vertical | Highest if specialised workspaces prove useful |
| Integrated modelling platform | Can eventually own calculations, scenarios, collaboration and audit inside one environment | Very high replacement cost and false claims of workbook compatibility | Low | Unknown; requires evidence that native-tool integration remains inadequate |

The recommended sequence is not a compromise between these forms. The first proof is the specialised model-change workspace. It lives inside a continuing company job. Excel is the first artifact adapter. Each layer remains replaceable and separately testable.

Evidence that would change the recommendation includes:

- analysts prefer reviewing and correcting directly in Excel and reject the web workspace;
- native integration cannot expose sufficient provenance or deterministic control;
- the conceptual object is more naturally expressed in an integrated model than around a workbook;
- continuing company context adds little value beyond isolated tasks;
- update-existing-model proves less useful than adjustments or earnings-derived structure; or
- review burden remains close to manual reconstruction.

## 5. Professional interaction grammar

The visible product uses a small grammar of professional acts. It does not expose runtime lifecycle or backend stage names.

### Begin or resume work

The analyst begins with an event, question, model, assumption, or change they are trying to make. The product may recover the company and current artifact from prior work. It asks for the missing professional facts, not a run instruction.

Visible concepts:

- company or security;
- what changed or what the analyst is trying to understand;
- current model or research artifact;
- intended use;
- evidence cutoff when material.

The analyst should not author a prompt such as “What should Codex produce?” The system compiles the professional intent into bounded work.

### Confirm the system's understanding

Before consequential work, the system presents one concise proposed interpretation:

```text
You are updating Micron FY2025 consolidated revenue in USD millions.
The current model value is 36,900. This input feeds revenue growth and the displayed revenue multiple.
The line appears to use reported annual GAAP revenue once the annual report is available.
```

The actions are `Confirm`, `Correct`, or `This is not the change I mean`.

This is not approval of a value, source, plan, or artifact. It creates or revises the conceptual-model binding.

### Authorise professional work

The product states the consequence, not the internal topology:

```text
Check the official annual sources, propose the historical update, and show every declared downstream change. Do not alter the original model.
```

The analyst can run, narrow, or cancel this work. The product chooses whether planning, one worker, separate branches, or independent review is necessary under an inspectable execution policy.

### Work quietly

The analyst should not click `Check readiness`, `Send exact job`, `Collect result`, or watch panes. The host handles capacity, dispatch, completion, retries, custody, and trace collection. Operational failure appears only when it changes what the analyst can do or trust.

### Interrupt on a material exception

An interruption is justified when professional meaning, method, source fit, unsupported artifact behaviour, or use authority cannot be resolved safely.

The exception should state:

- the affected professional object;
- what the system attempted;
- the exact mismatch or uncertainty;
- whether the artifact changed;
- what depends on the unresolved object; and
- the consequence of each available action.

Example:

```text
This value matches the annual total, but the proposal cites the September earnings release.
This model line is set to use the filed annual report once available.
The 10-K reports the same value. No workbook has been changed.

Use the 10-K source | Keep the current model value | Correct what this line represents
```

### Review a candidate

A usable candidate view shows only what changed and what still matters:

- original and candidate artifact identity;
- before and after values or formulas;
- exact source, calculation, estimate, or assumption;
- declared downstream changes;
- calculation status and unsupported features;
- remaining material uncertainty;
- named use awaiting decision.

The actions are `Use for [named use]`, `Amend`, `Reject`, or `Open the candidate in the native tool` when supported.

### Resume and inspect history

The job resumes at the current professional state. History is organised as artifact versions, decisions, exceptions, and corrections relevant to the current object. It is not a generic activity feed.

## 6. Joined frontend/backend architecture

The architecture has six joined planes. They are implementation boundaries, not primary navigation.

### 6.1 Professional job plane

Purpose: preserve the ongoing company or mandate context and select the current professional objective.

Canonical objects:

- `Tenant` and `Membership`;
- `ResearchJob` as the ongoing owned company or mandate context;
- `JobObjectiveVersion` for one exact question, change, named use, and cutoff;
- `WorkEpisode` for one immutable execution population inside the continuing job.

Existing substrate:

- authentication and director ownership exist;
- `ResearchCampaign` currently combines job and episode concerns;
- exact job fields and restart retrieval exist;
- separate job and episode semantics are proposed.

Visible consequence: the analyst resumes the company work and sees the current objective and artifact, while execution finality remains per episode rather than freezing the whole job.

### 6.2 Conceptual-model and authority plane

Purpose: join a native artifact target to economic meaning, method, assumptions, and human authority.

Canonical objects:

- `ConceptualObjectVersion` implementing `model-change-concept/v0`;
- `ArtifactBindingVersion` and bounded dependency closure;
- `EconomicBindingVersion`;
- `MethodBindingVersion`;
- `MeaningDisposition` with `CONFIRM_MEANING`, `AMEND_MEANING`, or `REJECT`;
- `MethodAuthorisation` for an exact reported, derived, estimate, adjustment, assumption, or scenario method; and
- `ArtifactUseDisposition` for one exact candidate and named use.

Existing substrate:

- the conceptual contract is committed but not implemented;
- proposal and research-state dispositions exist but do not represent these distinct authorities;
- a generic approval must not be extended to cover them.

Visible consequence: the system can ask a small number of precise questions instead of forcing the analyst to specify backend contracts or review an opaque result.

### 6.3 Evidence and entitlement plane

Purpose: make source identity, access, assertion, and cutoff machine-owned where deterministic evidence is available.

Canonical objects:

- `SourceDocumentVersion` for exact captured bytes and source metadata;
- `SourceAssertion` for one exact value or passage within a version;
- `EntitlementReceipt` recording which operating identity and route lawfully accessed the source;
- `SourcePolicyVersion` bound to the conceptual object and method;
- `AdmissibilityDecision` over exact host facts and attributed human policy; and
- explicit `unknown`, `unsupported`, `contested`, and `refused` results.

Existing substrate:

- uploaded bytes and digests exist;
- external search is not captured as exact source versions;
- model-authored source receipts remain self-certifying;
- source-to-target admission is absent.

Visible consequence: the analyst sees a source mismatch or ambiguity only when it affects the object. They do not manage a provenance console.

### 6.4 Work and cognition plane

Purpose: compile the professional objective into the smallest bounded agent work that can change the object, reduce uncertainty, or refuse.

Canonical objects:

- `WorkPlanVersion` only when planning adds value;
- `WorkUnitSpec` with professional objective, exact inputs, allowed tools and sources, output contract, refusal conditions, dependencies, budget, and claim ceiling;
- `CognitionPackageVersion` joining prompt, role, workbench, skill, model, tool grant, and context policy;
- `ExecutionEpisode` or the existing exact `WorkOrder` and runtime chronology;
- `AgentProposal` referring only to host-issued source, object, method, and artifact identities; and
- `ChallengeDecision` over one exact proposal or state.

Existing substrate:

- proposals, dispositions, work orders, version-pinned protocols, external skill locks, NTM/Codex execution, output attestation, and state transitions exist;
- arbitrary bound candidate content, mandatory reasoning operators, and worker-authored evidence identity remain defects.

Visible consequence: the analyst authorises professional work once. The host chooses the minimum execution topology and records it for later diagnosis.

### 6.5 Native artifact and correction plane

Purpose: create exact candidate descendants, calculate them, expose changed consequences, and apply attributed corrections without rewriting history.

Canonical objects:

- `NativeArtifactVersion` and adapter manifest;
- `ArtifactOperationProposal` restricted by the current adapter;
- `CandidateArtifactVersion` with exact parent and admitted proposal set;
- `CalculationReceipt` recording engine, version, errors, before/after values, formulas, and dependencies;
- `Amendment` referencing the exact object corrected and attributed actor;
- `InvalidationEvent` over descendants affected by ancestor change; and
- new candidate and use disposition after every material amendment.

Existing substrate:

- exact artifact bytes and the bounded workbook capability spike exist;
- product-level parsing, candidate creation, supported recalculation, invalidation, correction, and named use do not.

Visible consequence: the analyst receives an exact candidate artifact and can understand what changed without rebuilding the model from scratch.

### 6.6 Evaluation and harness-evolution plane

Purpose: turn exact corrections and failures into protected improvement experiments rather than live self-modification.

Canonical objects:

- `CorrectionRecord` anchored to exact inputs, proposal, decision, artifact, and human act;
- `FailureAttribution` identifying the responsible layer;
- `EvaluationCaseVersion` with immutable treatment and expected behaviour;
- `InterventionCandidateVersion` for prompt, skill, tool, representation, validator, topology, or procedure;
- `EvaluationRun` comparing baseline and candidate under the same conditions;
- `PromotionDecision` with scope, approver, rollback, and protected-case results; and
- `HarnessReleaseVersion` joining the promoted component versions.

Existing substrate:

- blind review can record corrections, evaluation candidates, and quarantined future proposals;
- they are not joined to owned artifacts, protected comparison, promotion, or rollback.

Visible consequence: the analyst corrects the current work once. The product does not ask them to design the harness. Their correction becomes an improvement candidate only when the causal relation is preserved.

## 7. Complete end-to-end job

### 7.1 Login, tenancy and resumption

The analyst authenticates into one tenant. Every job, source, artifact, execution identity, correction, and evaluation case is tenant-bound. Tenant lookup remains fail-closed and unrelated users receive no existence disclosure.

The initial surface is search or recent company work, not a dashboard of system states. Opening a company returns the current professional objective, latest relied-upon artifacts, unresolved exceptions, and one next meaningful action.

### 7.2 Begin from work, not administration

The analyst states what changed or what they need to do and attaches or selects the current native artifact. The system captures a job objective version and exact artifact version. Evidence cutoff and named use are inferred only as proposals and confirmed when material.

### 7.3 Inspect and recover the conceptual object

The native adapter extracts structural facts. A model may propose economic meaning and method from labels, formulas, source context, and prior confirmed bindings. Host facts and model proposals remain separate. The product presents one bounded understanding for confirmation.

Unknown or contested material fields prevent proposal admission. The product may continue safe preparatory work, but must not silently complete the conceptual object.

### 7.4 Capture sources and entitlement

Sources enter through host-owned capture routes. Exact bytes, URLs, access time, source identity, entitlement route, and assertions are stored. The model may interpret assertions but cannot issue the source identity used to validate itself.

A source unavailable under the user's entitlement becomes a visible access limitation, not an invented negative result.

### 7.5 Compile and authorise bounded work

The host compiles the objective, conceptual object, sources, methods, artifact facts, tools, and budgets into the smallest work unit. Planning is conditional. Different branches are created only for genuinely distinct evidence, context, permission, or challenge requirements.

The analyst authorises the professional consequence. The exact work-unit packet, cognition versions, and runtime identity remain inspectable in support records.

### 7.6 Dispatch through NTM/Codex

The current NTM/Codex spine is retained. Runtime entitlement remains distinct from product login and research authority. Each commercial deployment will need a lawful isolated operating identity rather than multiplexing the founder's subscription.

The worker receives host-issued identities and returns typed proposals or bounded refusal. It cannot create source identity, artifact identity, human meaning, or use authority.

### 7.7 Admit, block, or reserve for judgment

Deterministic validators check exact identities, target dimensions, source policy, operation, cutoff, units, supported artifact state, and legal transition. A known mismatch blocks before artifact mutation. A professional ambiguity becomes an exception requiring attributed judgment.

The product distinguishes:

- machine-invalid;
- unsupported;
- professionally ambiguous;
- admissible candidate; and
- refused work.

### 7.8 Create and calculate a candidate artifact

Only admitted operations apply to a new descendant. The original remains immutable. A supported calculation engine produces an exact calculation receipt. Formula errors, unsupported features, changed references, missing dependencies, or engine failure refuse the candidate.

The resulting candidate is provisional even after successful calculation.

### 7.9 Review by exception

The analyst sees the professional delta, support, downstream consequences, unresolved assumptions, and use awaiting decision. Quietly valid work is not expanded into a compliance dossier. Every exception states what the analyst's action will cause.

### 7.10 Amend and recalculate

An amendment never rewrites the original worker proposal or candidate. It creates a new conceptual binding, method, source selection, assumption, or operation version. Descendant use is invalidated. A new candidate and calculation receipt are required.

### 7.11 Permit reliance

`USE_CANDIDATE` binds one exact candidate to one tenant, job, scenario, named use, actor, and time. It does not grant universal truth, publication, or investment authority. `REJECT` and `REQUEST_REWORK` retain chronology.

### 7.12 Restart, audit and continue

The same job, artifacts, sources, meanings, methods, exceptions, executions, corrections, decisions, and use scope are available after restart. Operational traces remain diagnostic and cannot change product state.

### 7.13 Convert corrections into evaluated change

The correction loop is explicit:

```text
exact analyst correction
-> causal failure attribution
-> candidate change at the responsible layer
-> protected held-out evaluation
-> regression, artifact-integrity and review-cost comparison
-> scoped promotion or rollback
```

No raw feedback, trace volume, model agreement, or single successful case changes the live harness.

## 8. Agent and topology policy

The product story is not “watch agents work.” Agents are implementation resources selected by context and evidence needs.

Use one capable worker by default. Introduce a separate planner only when the objective has material ambiguity, multiple costly routes, or branch dependencies. Use a fresh adversarial context only when a named failure can change admission, claim scope, or next work. Use synthesis only over an exact selected support set when multiple work products must be joined. Machine judgment remains an evaluation sidecar, never analyst authorship.

A topology candidate must state:

- why shared context is harmful or useful;
- which inputs are separated;
- which output can change;
- how independence is technically supported;
- the extra reconciliation cost; and
- the evaluation that would justify retention.

## 9. Visible product composition

The product should not default to a sidebar, dashboard, card grid, campaign list, or pipeline. A coherent first composition is:

- a company/work search and recent-resumption entry;
- one current objective and artifact context;
- the system's bounded understanding when confirmation is required;
- the current work or material exception;
- the resulting candidate and consequences;
- contextual versions and decisions; and
- support-only technical evidence behind an explicit diagnostic route.

Richness comes from the model, source, method, assumptions, change, consequences, and artifact—not from panels or navigation density.

## 11. Revised build strategy

The existing backend-first Slice 1 framing is no longer sufficient. Future implementation must proceed in outcome-complete verticals whose frontend action and backend consequence arrive together.

### Vertical A — Understand and confirm one model object

One uploaded synthetic model is structurally inspected. The system proposes one bounded economic and method binding. The user can confirm or correct it. Canonical state records host facts, model proposal, and human disposition separately. No model mutation or source admission is required.

Falsifier: the analyst cannot understand or correct the proposed object more easily than authoring the metadata manually.

### Vertical B — Contain one wrong proposal

The product captures the exact Micron source pair, dispatches one real bounded Codex work unit, receives the controlled wrong-source proposal, and blocks it before artifact mutation. The exception appears in ordinary analyst language and every visible statement maps to canonical state.

Falsifier: the gate requires episode-specific hard-coding, overblocks a legitimate alternative target, or the exception does not reduce review burden.

### Vertical C — Correct, recalculate and permit use

An attributed correction selects the admissible source, creates a new proposal, patches a candidate, calculates it through the selected supported adapter, presents changed dependencies, and records exact use or rejection.

Falsifier: recalculation is unreliable, correction cannot produce an exact descendant, or the analyst still reconstructs the whole model manually.

### Vertical D — Turn one correction into an evaluated harness candidate

The exact source correction becomes an evaluation case. One candidate intervention is compared against the trigger and protected different cases. Promotion and rollback are explicit.

Falsifier: the system cannot locate the responsible layer or the intervention improves the trigger while worsening held-out cases or review cost.

No implementation packet is authorised by this architecture document. The active direct Excel assumption test and queued classifier repair remain bounded enabling investigations. Product construction requires a later explicit decision after hostile review of the joined contracts.

## 12. Nonclaims and falsifier

This architecture does not establish that the conceptual contract is complete, that analysts prefer a web workspace, that Excel is the correct first adapter, that the current repository can support the proposed source or artifact operations, or that the business will pay for the product.

The recommendation should be killed or materially revised when a cold analyst cannot:

- recognise the job without learning internal vocabulary;
- correct the system's understanding with less effort than reconstructing the work;
- explain why a proposal was blocked;
- understand what changed and what depends on it;
- distinguish machine fact, model proposal, and human judgment;
- obtain a reliable candidate artifact; or
- state exactly what their final action authorises.
