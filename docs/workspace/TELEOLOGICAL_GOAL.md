# Teleological goal

Status: controlling product and engineering goal for the next implementation.

## Completion object

A sophisticated analyst can state broad professional work in ordinary language inside a configured local workspace. Persistent Codex sessions created through NTM can investigate, compare, calculate, challenge, and create candidate artifacts across several turns. The local research service preserves the exact professional commission, evidence, context, checkpoints, claims, artifacts, corrections, decisions, and later replay. The analyst receives useful work and the few matters that still require judgment without reconstructing every retrieval and transformation.

The completed system should support this passage:

```text
broad analyst request
-> confirmed professional commission
-> durable research branches
-> persistent NTM-managed Codex sessions
-> host-mediated source capture and context additions
-> branch checkpoints and resumption
-> fresh challenge where independence matters
-> typed claims and candidate artifacts
-> analyst correction and scoped use
-> later episode beginning from the corrected state
-> NTM-managed independent re-derivation
```

Codex performs model-mediated research. NTM creates and maintains the persistent Codex sessions. The local research service owns professional and evidential authority.

## Product reason

Useful agents lower the cost of research, model work, comparison, and artifact production. Lower cost causes more work across more companies, sources, methods, and users. Plausible mistakes can then travel farther while exhaustive review removes the economic gain.

The product succeeds when routine work remains cheap, consequential uncertainty reaches the analyst, corrections do not require restarting the undertaking, and later work begins from the right state.

Exact provenance has no independent product value when the analyst still rebuilds the result.

## Local minima to leave

### Deterministic worker as the research system

A deterministic worker can test context, storage, result, and replay contracts. It cannot establish useful research, branch-local continuity, discovery, challenge, or professional judgment.

The deterministic runner remains a conformance tool. It is not the Codex runtime.

### One dispatch and one output

Research often changes route after new evidence, an analyst correction, or a challenge. A one-shot worker contract cannot represent a long-lived inquiry without hiding the state changes inside an opaque model transcript.

A durable branch with checkpoints replaces the one-shot worker as the unit of research.

### NTM completion as research completion

A tracked send proves transport. An NTM completion response describes session state. Neither proves that Codex understood the branch, preserved the right evidence relations, checkpointed its state, or returned a valid candidate.

Branch completion requires semantic acknowledgement, checkpoint, typed result or refusal, exact artifacts, and host custody.

### Terminal history as memory

A persistent pane retains useful working context. It also contains abandoned interpretations, raw tool output, and model-authored conclusions. Terminal history belongs to execution evidence, not professional authority.

Durable checkpoints and confirmed perspective items carry later work.

### Multi-agent topology as product

Several Codex sessions may earn their place through different evidence, tools, write authority, fresh judgment, or useful parallel work. Session count does not establish evidence count.

The analyst sees the professional consequence, not a graph of panes.

### The Micron cell as product centre

The equal-value source case tests one dangerous relation. It remains a protected case inside a broader workspace for research, calculations, native artifacts, monitoring, and institutional memory.

### Audit without reduced review

Hashes, schemas, and lineages can coexist with useless or burdensome work. The system must measure analyst review time, manual reconstruction, useful artifact adoption, false blocks, unsafe routes, consultant repair, and repeated use.

## Durable research branch

A research branch exists before one Codex session starts and survives after that session stops.

The branch records:

- parent episode and exact commission;
- prior perspective and professional question;
- method and skill versions;
- allowed evidence and tools;
- authority ceiling;
- NTM session attempts;
- exact instructions and acknowledgements;
- host-mediated context additions;
- append-only checkpoints;
- claims, contradictions, calculations, and artifact proposals;
- unresolved professional questions;
- result or refusal;
- later disposition; and
- replay relations.

One branch may require several NTM session attempts. One NTM session may disappear without destroying the branch.

## Persistent Codex session

An NTM-managed Codex session supplies live working continuity.

The session may:

- inspect the starting context;
- call approved tools;
- discover source candidates;
- request host capture;
- receive admitted context additions;
- revise the research route;
- submit checkpoints;
- request professional judgment;
- produce claims and candidate artifacts; and
- stop with a bounded refusal.

The session may not create source identity, extend its own authority, promote its conclusions into active memory, or grant use of its artifacts.

No `codex exec` or second Codex control plane is authorised.

## Human role

The analyst or an authorised standing policy supplies authority where the system cannot determine the answer mechanically.

The human may confirm or amend:

- the intended professional use;
- material professional-object meaning;
- source and method rules;
- assumptions;
- materiality;
- treatment of unresolved contradiction;
- use of an exact claim or artifact;
- scope of active memory; and
- promotion of a tested method.

The interface should ask only where the answer would change consequential work.

## First product proof

The first live proof should use one lead session, one persistent investigation session, one fresh challenge session, and one fresh re-derivation session. Every Codex session should be created and maintained through NTM.

The proof should include:

- one exact prior perspective;
- one broad analyst request;
- discovery of at least one source not preassembled into the answer;
- host capture and purpose-specific admission;
- at least two research turns;
- one durable checkpoint before completion;
- one challenge that can narrow or change a claim;
- one useful candidate artifact;
- one analyst correction;
- one scoped use decision;
- a second episode using the correction within scope; and
- independent re-derivation from the historical evidence cutoff.

The proof fails when a deterministic fixture stands in for Codex, NTM session state stands in for a research result, a consultant silently repairs the output, or the analyst reconstructs most of the work.

## Commercial hurdle

The first commercial experiment should seek either:

```text
same professional scope
-> at least 50 percent less analyst time from request to usable artifact
```

or:

```text
same analyst review hours
-> at least twice the useful company or question coverage
```

The exact threshold remains a hypothesis. The product must nevertheless set a hurdle before controls, integrations, and another interface accumulate without measurable leverage.
