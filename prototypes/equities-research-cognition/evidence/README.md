# Public evidence boundary

The private source repository retains small historical receipts and
counterexamples. Their JSON is intentionally absent from this public export
because it contains service-issued project, trace, run, session, and job
identifiers and belongs to earlier implementation lanes.

The executable mechanical regressions remain under `scenarios/adversarial/`.
They can establish deterministic package behavior for the exact cases they
exercise. They do not prove a fresh complete research campaign, research
correctness, analyst usefulness, intervention effect, product fit, or protected
improvement.

Any future public receipt should be purpose-created, independently reviewed,
redacted, tied to an exact public commit, and accompanied by explicit
nonclaims.
