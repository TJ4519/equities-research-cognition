## Scope

The package contains the inherited Django/PostgreSQL product path and the new
local-first research workspace kernel.

The inherited path provides campaign ownership, exact input custody, NTM/Codex
control, provisional outputs, human actions, Langfuse readback, and mechanical
regressions.

source assertions, purpose-specific evidence decisions, sealed assertion-level
contexts, a direct replaceable process boundary, host-validated claims and
artifacts, scoped decisions, correction, memory, and inference-state replay.

adoption by inference.
