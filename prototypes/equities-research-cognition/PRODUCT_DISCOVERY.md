# Equities Research Product Discovery

Status: open product-discovery ground, not implementation authority.

This document preserves the governing question and unresolved discriminators
that a scope, product, cognition, or architecture reviewer must carry. It does
not select a product, authorize a campaign, create research truth, or replace
the repository's completion ceiling.

## Governing question

What differentiated and commercially legible product becomes possible when
frontier agents can perform explicit equities-research craft, while ordinary
expert use generates the judgments needed to improve that craft?

Campaign Studio, NTM, Langfuse, PostgreSQL, role protocols, workbenches, and
memory mechanisms are candidate means. None is the product proposition by
itself.

## Completion hypothesis to test

An expert should be able to use the prototype as a serious research agent:

```text
material question and decision context
-> inspectable and revisable research programme
-> live information foraging, calculation, sensemaking, challenge, synthesis
-> investor-native artifact or bounded refusal
-> expert annotations on consequential research decisions
-> scoped candidate memories or capability changes
-> separate human acceptance of one reversible experiment
-> later comparable evaluation
-> helped | harmed | inconclusive
```

The research result and the judgeable trajectory are two outputs of the same
ordinary use. A trajectory becomes learning evidence only after an identified
expert authors a judgment under the governed chronology.

## Distinctions that must survive

- A source document is an input artifact, not a case or a judgment.
- A trigger or schedule starts work; it is not the research episode.
- A campaign is an execution envelope around a commission and evidence cutoff;
  it is not automatically the natural unit of fund work.
- Research state is the evolving evidence, alternatives, uncertainties, claims,
  and decision consequences that may persist across work episodes.
- An expert interaction may be clarification, local correction, preference,
  reusable-method candidate, evaluation candidate, or authority act. These
  cannot be promoted interchangeably.
- Episodic memory preserves exact prior experience. Semantic memory expresses
  a scoped lesson. Procedural memory changes a skill or workbench. Protected
  evaluation tests whether an accepted change helped. Retrieval alone is not
  judgment distillation.
- A skill is a fallible transformation to test, not a badge proving cognition.
- Multi-agent topology is a task-shaped hypothesis, not a synonym for agent
  count or a fixed product ontology.

## Open product questions

1. Which actual fund jobs produce both immediate decision value and dense,
   reusable expert judgment?
2. What native artifact is consumed next: ranked queue, spreadsheet/model
   delta, reconciliation, chart, thesis-state update, scenario table, memo,
   alert, or refusal?
3. Which expert decisions are stable and consequential enough to distil, and
   which holistic judgments would be damaged by forced decomposition?
4. What should remain local episode memory, organization-specific semantic
   memory, procedural skill change, or protected evaluation material?
5. How should Research Frame, information foraging and sensemaking, Modes of
   Reasoning, equities workbenches, tools, and topology combine so each
   invocation has an observable epistemic or decision delta?
6. Which missing skills should be developed from observed expert failures
   rather than invented as a catalogue?
7. When does one strong agent suffice, and when do persistent specialist
   contexts improve independence, context economy, route-back, or artifact
   quality?
8. Which parts of an approved research run must remain portable across
   Codex/NTM, native Codex delegation, and OMP/IDE execution, and which
   lifecycle, identity, tool, model, output-custody, and telemetry capabilities
   must a harness prove before the product offers it?
9. How should annotations attach to exact research state without turning every
   interaction, trace, preference, or model agreement into authority?
10. Which product form provides immediate analyst utility while bootstrapping a
   governed institutional research memory?
11. What buyer, adoption path, integration surface, privacy boundary, and
    measurable payoff correspond to the surviving hypothesis?

Candidate workloads such as attention triage, earnings/model revision,
persistent coverage, material investigation, and investment-committee
preparation are discovery inputs. They are not yet separate products or a
mandatory workflow taxonomy.

The current executable supports one server-wide Codex/NTM configuration rather
than a selectable runtime profile. OMP is installed and exposes persistent
sessions, explicit model/tool configuration, RPC, and editor-facing ACP, but it
has not been integrated or exercised through this product. A model or harness
selector would therefore be a false capability until one material run proves
the same approved work identity, exact output custody, lifecycle facts, and
trajectory correlation through that runtime.

## Immediate lived-use test

Before expanding the product ontology or memory system, experience the accepted
prototype through one material, lawful research episode.

The ordinary user must be able to:

- commission research in investor decision language;
- understand, reject, revise, rewrite, or authorize the proposed programme;
- observe how evidence changes rivals, uncertainties, claims, next routes, and
  stopping decisions;
- receive useful calculations, charts, models, prose, or an honest refusal;
- annotate the exact claim, source, assumption, calculation, omission, or route
  on which expert judgment differs;
- see what local correction or future change is being proposed from that
  judgment; and
- accept or reject any experimental change separately.

A running server, spawned pane, sophisticated prompt, skill invocation,
Langfuse trace, captured artifact, polished page, green test, or model
agreement does not satisfy this test.

## First judgment-distillation proof

Do not build clustering or automatic lesson promotion first.

```text
one material episode
-> one exact expert correction
-> one evidence-linked candidate lesson
-> one scoped, reversible accepted change
-> later comparable work
-> helped | harmed | inconclusive
```

The candidate lesson must retain its triggering episode, scope, supporting and
contradicting evidence, author, proposed target, rollback, and authority state.
Until later evaluation, the strongest claim is that the product captured and
applied an expert-authorized experiment—not that the system improved.

## Anti-repeat rule

Do not re-churn this discovery question into another explanation, governance
layer, fixed case schema, Langfuse gate, dashboard, skill catalogue, or
orchestration programme. A retained mechanism must improve a lived research
episode, preserve an otherwise-lost expert judgment, or enable one governed
later comparison.
