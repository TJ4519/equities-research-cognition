# Equities research synthesis

## Purpose

Combine an exact director-selected set of accepted research states into one
useful investor-facing research read or bounded refusal.

Synthesis should reveal what becomes visible only when the branches are
considered together: a cross-branch mechanism, contradiction, expectation
absorption, recognition path, observability limit or reason to stop. It is not
an exercise in concatenating branch summaries.

Run this protocol in a fresh context over the exact selected support set.

## Case-specific input

Use only:

- the exact approved synthesis proposal and `packet.contract`;
- materialized `inputs`;
- `research_state_input`, if lawfully supplied; and
- the exact `supporting_research_states` selected for this work order.

The host verifies that each selected support is an active accepted leaf. It does
not currently prove that every active leaf was selected. Unless an exact
host-issued closure artifact is present in `inputs`, label the basis:

```text
SELECTED_SUPPORT_SET_NOT_CAMPAIGN_COMPLETE
```

Do not inspect ambient campaign state or certify global completeness.

An accepted research state may contain candidate rival and claim-impact
histories. Acceptance of the state does not make those histories operative
claim transitions.

## Authority

You may:

- compose accepted observations and typed workbench results;
- compare and split candidate interpretations;
- identify cross-branch mechanisms and contradictions;
- produce bounded candidate claims inside the weakest relevant ceiling;
- expose decision implications without selecting the action; and
- nominate one next discriminator, stop or refusal for later planning.

You may not:

- retrieve new evidence;
- repair or rewrite a workbench result or supporting state;
- recreate source provenance;
- count same-origin support as independent;
- promote a worker claim history into an operative license;
- infer an action threshold or no-action region;
- select an investment action;
- publish an authoritative conclusion; or
- close the campaign.

The output is a pre-authority synthesis candidate.

## Synthesis loop

### 1. Reconstruct the exact support basis

For every selected state, preserve:

- transition and artifact identity;
- evidence cutoff;
- exact observations and source-origin limits;
- workbench-native result, assumption, residual, conflict or refusal;
- candidate rival and claim impacts; and
- immutable planner/workbench claim ceiling.

If a load-bearing object is missing, narrow or refuse. Do not repair the chain
with narrative coherence. The current state packet does not reliably carry the
producing case-frame and branch commitments; when they are not materialized in
`inputs`, mark `HOST_GAP_FRAME_LINEAGE_NOT_MATERIALIZED` rather than
reconstructing them.

### 2. Find joint analytical value

Ask what the branches jointly reveal that none establishes alone.

Look for:

- an operating mechanism supported across distinct evidence surfaces;
- a contradiction between reported performance, operating drivers and public
  expectations;
- a distinction between company fundamentals and what may already be absorbed
  by guidance, consensus, narrative, positioning or model-implied routes;
- a recognition path by which a bounded fundamental difference could matter;
- a serious rival that survives all branches; or
- a public-evidence limit that makes refusal more useful than further prose.

Do not force a positive insight. A precise underidentification or refusal is a
valid joint result.

### 3. Preserve workbench boundaries

Do not:

- use W1 comparability as a causal explanation;
- turn W2 units into demand, `price_mix` into pricing power, shipments into
  sell-through or a residual into a mechanism;
- collapse W3 guidance, consensus, narrative, positioning and model-implied
  routes into one market expectation; or
- turn W4's current `DECISION_AUTHORITY_UNAVAILABLE` into a no-action result.

A stale source surface is a limitation inside an accepted state, not proof that
the whole state is stale.

### 4. Compose candidate claims without claim authority

For every material candidate claim, show:

- what exact accepted observation or typed result supports it;
- which source-origin family carries the support;
- which live rival it strengthens, weakens or leaves unresolved;
- the relevant proxy gap;
- the weakest governing claim ceiling;
- blocked stronger language; and
- what would update the read.

Where branches support different parts of a broad sentence, split the sentence.
Do not majority-vote across branches.

### 5. Make contradictions and refusals decision-focused

Foreground every contradiction, refusal or missing discriminator that changes:

- the candidate conclusion;
- the investor-relevant research priority;
- the claim ceiling;
- the need for more work; or
- the justification to stop.

Preserve other diagnostics in a compact audit appendix rather than dumping them
all into the main read.

### 6. Bound decision implications

State what the evidence may change in the investor's view, model or diligence
priority. Do not choose the investment action.

Without an exact authorised Decision-Consequence map, say that investment
action mapping was not evaluated. Preserve an exact
`DECISION_AUTHORITY_UNAVAILABLE` result when supplied.

### 7. Name the lawful next state

End with one of:

- bounded synthesis candidate ready for final review;
- one non-authoritative next-discriminator candidate for planner re-entry;
- stop because further public work has no decision value; or
- refusal because the required construct cannot be separated with lawful
  evidence.

A next discriminator must name the unresolved state, possible result effects
and kill condition. It does not self-dispatch.

## Main human artifact: `synthesis.md`

Write an Investment Research Read with:

1. **Bottom line**
2. **What changed the working view**
3. **The operating or causal mechanism supported, if any**
4. **What expectations may already absorb**
5. **Live alternatives and contradictions**
6. **What is known, underidentified or not publicly observable**
7. **Decision implications within current authority**
8. **What the evidence cannot establish**
9. **Next discriminator, stop or refusal**
10. **Support and claim-boundary appendix**
11. **Host gaps**

The main read should be understandable without the appendix. The appendix
preserves exact transition, artifact and observation references, source-origin
limits, candidate claim mappings and ceilings.

Label the result:

```text
CANDIDATE_PENDING_FINAL_REVIEW
```

This label does not admit review or make the result authoritative.

## Return path and host gaps

The intended path is:

```text
exact synthesis candidate
-> adversarial final review
-> human / claim-authority disposition
```

The current host does not automatically admit a synthesis artifact to final
review and has no operative campaign claim chronology. Mark:

- `HOST_GAP_POST_SYNTHESIS_REVIEW_ADMISSION`; and
- `HOST_GAP_OPERATIVE_CLAIM_DISPOSITION`

where relevant. Do not say that the candidate passed review, became an accepted
claim or was published.
