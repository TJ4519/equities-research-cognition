# DeepResearch candidate evaluation rubric

## Purpose

Evaluate whether the candidate protocols cause better equities research moves,
not whether they look more rigorous or produce cleaner schemas.

Judge three outcomes separately:

1. **research quality**: did the system reduce the right investor uncertainty
   through useful evidence, analysis, adaptation or refusal?
2. **custody and handoff**: can the exact material trajectory be reconstructed
   without inventing authority?
3. **expert-learning value**: can a qualified analyst identify the exact choice,
   available cue, better transition and decision impact?

A high score in one category does not prove either of the others.

## Proof classes

### Mechanical checks

Schema validation, hashes, required files, exact set equality, legal state
transitions, parser fixtures and rendered pages are mechanical evidence.

They receive **zero cognition credit**. They may block a run for invalid
custody, but they cannot establish research quality, expert usefulness or
improvement.

### Material episode evidence

A research-quality comparison requires a real equities commission with:

- investor use and frozen public cutoff;
- production model, tools, permissions and workbenches;
- live source selection and information foraging;
- an adaptive research state;
- exact human and model actions;
- a bounded result or refusal; and
- later qualified-human judgment.

A failed material episode remains evidence about the path. Do not replace it
with a toy or preassembled packet and call that cognition validation.

## Controlled active-versus-candidate comparison

Hold fixed:

- model and reasoning setting;
- natural-language commission and decision use;
- evidence cutoff;
- source permissions and tools;
- resource budget and stopping policy;
- available workbench versions;
- host commit and transport rules; and
- human evaluation protocol.

Use fresh contexts for active and candidate arms.

Do **not** freeze:

- source choice or order;
- branch choice;
- route adaptation;
- context accumulation;
- challenge response;
- stopping; or
- refusal.

Those are research outcomes.

Where the planner itself is not the treatment, freeze one exact approved
upstream programme before comparing downstream protocols. Otherwise planner
variance confounds the interface effect.

## Scoring

Score each dimension from 0 to 3:

- `0`: absent, harmful or authority-violating;
- `1`: present in prose but generic or non-causal;
- `2`: materially functional with clear limits;
- `3`: strong enough to change a real decision or expert judgment and survive
  exact review.

Do not average away a critical fail.

### 1. Decision interpretation

- Does the planner recover the investor decision or belief update rather than a
  topic?
- Are target, proxy gap, horizon, cutoff and costly error clear?
- Can the director correct the interpretation in ordinary language?
- Does an exact lookup remain simple when that is all the commission asks?

### 2. Positive analytical yield

- Does the programme seek observations that discriminate serious rivals?
- Does it discover a useful operating variable, mechanism, source route,
  expectation difference, recognition path or observability limit that a
  strong baseline misses?
- Does synthesis reveal something material only visible across branches?
- Does review catch underlicensing and decision-useless caution as well as
  overclaim?

More branches, sources, modes or prose do not count as yield.

### 3. Source and evidence discipline

- Are sources point-in-time and inside the cutoff?
- Are source surface and source origin kept separate?
- Are same-origin retellings collapsed for warrant but preserved for custody?
- Are exact observations distinguished from reasoning and route changes?
- Is absence interpreted only through a visibility model?

### 4. Adaptive state change

- Before work, are plausible result classes tied to different state or
  programme consequences?
- After evidence, does the next route, rival set, claim boundary, branch,
  synthesis, stop or refusal change when warranted?
- Are results outside the prior outcome map surfaced rather than forced into it?
- Does a material result return through genuine planner re-entry rather than
  prose claiming that it did?

### 5. Claim and authority containment

- Are worker claim histories kept candidate-only?
- Does synthesis remain pre-authority?
- Does review recommend rather than repair or license?
- Are W1-W4 boundaries preserved?
- Does missing decision authority remain distinct from a no-action result?
- Are machine and analyst judgment kept separate?

Containment is necessary, but blanket refusal or unusable caution loses positive
yield credit.

### 6. Human legibility

- Can the director understand what is proposed, why it matters, what different
  results would do and what to correct without parsing JSON?
- Can the investor read the synthesis without first reading the audit appendix?
- Can the analyst understand the exact judgment question without reverse-
  engineering the full trajectory?

### 7. Handoff and host compatibility

- Does every role consume exact objects the host actually supplies?
- Do semantic outputs use current filenames and proposal vocabulary?
- Are unimplemented transitions marked `HOST_GAP` rather than narrated as
  achieved?
- Does the machine appendix preserve identity without becoming the human
  explanation?

### 8. Judgeability for expert correction

For every material choice, can a later reviewer recover:

- the question being answered;
- evidence and alternatives available at that time;
- the transition chosen;
- the cue expected to change it;
- the observed result; and
- the downstream consequence?

Private chain-of-thought is neither required nor credited.

### 9. Cost and latency proportionality

- Is task depth proportionate to the investor hinge and cost of error?
- Are zero-value routes parked?
- Is preparatory work explicitly tied to a downstream discriminator?
- Does the candidate avoid multi-agent or multi-mode ceremony?
- Does any quality gain justify added time, token, source and human-review cost?

### 10. Correct refusal

A refusal scores highly when it correctly states:

- what cannot be established;
- why the available public evidence, workbench or authority is insufficient;
- what would have to change;
- and why more work has no current decision value.

A refusal fails when it substitutes caution for available bounded insight or
uses a host limitation to avoid an otherwise lawful research move.

## Critical fails

Regardless of score, fail the candidate arm if it:

- treats planned work, a prior, mechanism card, ranking, model agreement or
  memory as case evidence;
- counts same-origin material as independent support;
- leaks post-cutoff or protected material;
- promotes a workbench result beyond its ceiling;
- dispatches a zero-value branch;
- presents preparatory work as the whole decision-discriminating programme;
- fabricates a positive observation or state change to satisfy the current
  schema;
- silently repairs an upstream state;
- treats accepted research state as accepted claim;
- turns synthesis or review into claim authority;
- invents a decision threshold or no-action region;
- exposes machine judgment before analyst lock;
- lets analyst correction become evidence or automatic policy;
- claims an unimplemented host transition occurred; or
- calls mechanical compliance research improvement.

## Role-specific checks

### Planner

The proposed programme must show why each branch is decision-discriminating or
what exact discriminator a preparatory branch unlocks. It must refuse when the
available workbench set cannot answer the commission rather than choose the
most executable table.

### Research worker

The worker must preserve attempt, source origin, observation, reasoning and
route decisions separately. An operator invocation counts only when its named
defect, exact inspection boundary, one mutable candidate output and realised
delta or kill are inspectable.

### Adversarial review

The review must produce `PASS | NARROW | BLOCK | RESEARCH`, preserve the
candidate unchanged, test the named defect and state an executable consequence.
Generic caution is a fail.

### Synthesis

The synthesis must produce a useful joint read, preserve contradictions and
ceilings, remain relative to the exact selected support set and stay
pre-authority.

### Machine judgment

Substantive judgment is a fail unless the host supplies and validates the exact
contract, closure, reference standard and exposure record. Under the current
host, the correct result is a typed `REFUSED` object.

## Required controls

### Positive control: Micron branch admission

Use the frozen Micron-like commission:

> Decide whether the company merits serious underwriting, a watchlist or no
> more time; distinguish durable HBM/earnings power from an ordinary memory
> cycle, mix/timing, supply/execution fade and already-priced scarcity; refuse
> if demand, mix, pricing, supply and timing cannot be separated.

The known failure is a programme whose only branch is a two-quarter consolidated
revenue reconstruction.

A passing candidate planner must either:

1. propose executable operating-state and expectation work whose possible
   returns change the underwriting/watchlist/no-more-time path; or
2. state that the current workbench set cannot perform the required analysis and
   refuse to substitute table reconstruction.

It must classify any revenue-comparability step as preparatory unless the
commission itself makes comparability the decision hinge.

### Negative control: simple comparability is sufficient

Use a commission whose only decision is:

> Can these two exact issuer-reported figures lawfully enter one comparable fact
> set at the frozen cutoff?

A passing planner may propose only Comparable-State work. It should not invent
operating mechanisms, expectation branches, valuation work or a downstream
programme. The result is decision-discriminating because comparability is the
commissioned uncertainty.

The Micron-derived rule fails if it forces unnecessary breadth in this control.

### Operator ablation

Where a reasoning operation is claimed to help, compare:

- the same case with the exact operation selected; and
- a fresh-context arm with no operation.

Hold the evidence, model and workbench fixed. Count only a material, named
state or decision delta that later survives human review. More prose or more
caution is not contribution.

## Evaluation output

Produce a human-readable comparison:

```text
Episode and treatment:
Proof class: mechanical | material episode
Active arm result:
Candidate arm result:
Critical fails:
Dimension scores and evidence:
Material research delta:
Custody delta:
Expert-learning delta:
Cost and latency delta:
Qualified-human judgment:
Required repair or no_material_update:
Scope of any provisional cutover:
What remains unproven:
```

One episode may support a bounded cutover, rollback or `no_material_update`.
It cannot establish general cognition improvement, analyst validation or durable
policy value.
